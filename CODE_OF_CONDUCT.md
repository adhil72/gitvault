# Code of Conduct

We want GitVault to be a welcoming place to work on. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block people who repeatedly break these rules.

To report a problem, email the maintainer through the contact address on their GitHub profile (https://github.com/adhil72). Reports are kept confidential.

This is a short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
